# Contributing

Thank you for helping make this list better!

1. **One resource per pull request**, added to the most fitting section.
2. Format: `- [Name](https://link) — one short line on why it is worth someone's time.`
3. **Free or free-to-audit only**, unless the paid resource is truly exceptional (mark it 💲).
4. Check that the link works and is the official source, not a re-upload or mirror.
5. No affiliate links, link shorteners or self-promotion without substance.

Spotted a broken or outdated link? Please [open an issue](../../issues/new) — that helps just as much.
